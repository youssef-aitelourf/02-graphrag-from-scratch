In September 2024, incident INC-2024-09 affected Nexus-400 units at the Marseille Orbit site. Meridian release r1841 widened a geofence polygon into a crane exclusion zone. Nexus drones entered a loiter pattern because the VEX collision-avoidance policy refused forward motion near crane structures.

Rollback to Meridian r1842 restored nominal throughput within 41 minutes. The Platform SRE postmortem states Priya Nair added a CI check blocking releases when polygon self-intersections are detected, after finding that r1841 slipped Canary because validation only covered rectangular fences.

Marco Silva confirmed on the Orbit call that Helix gateway masts on quay cranes were not at fault: telemetry showed geofence vertices sent by Meridian mismatched approved crane CAD boundaries from supplier data.
